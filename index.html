Good To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there parking available?

Yes! Merryland Pavilion has an on-site parking area, so no worries there.

Are kids welcome?

Absolutely, kids are welcome to celebrate with us.

Can I bring a plus one?

As much as we'd love to host everyone, our seating is limited, so plus ones aren't advisable unless it's stated on your invitation that you may bring one. We appreciate your understanding.

What time should I arrive?

Doors open at 2:45 PM. Please make sure to be there before 3:00 PM as the ceremony will begin promptly.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

Feel free to take photos and tag us — we'd love to see the day through your eyes too! We just kindly ask that you stay mindful of our photographer and videographer's sightlines, and avoid stepping in front of them during the ceremony.

Where can we stay nearby?

We recommend 8 Waves Waterpark Hotel and Resort, just about 30 minutes from the venue.
